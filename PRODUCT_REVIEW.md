# PMS product review for the Hotevo website

## Scope

Reviewed the signed-in Sofia PMS v2.7.5 interface available in the browser, including the hotel operations workspace and the separate administration area. This inventory describes capabilities visible in that instance. Module switches, permissions, payment setup and integrations can affect what is available to a specific property. No guest records, contact details, credentials or business-specific figures are intended for use in public marketing copy.

## Product capability inventory

### Reservations and availability

- Reservation list with search, status and arrival-date filters, configurable columns and CSV export.
- New reservations and walk-ins with a live quote; supports stay dates, room type, occupancy, meal plan, voucher, multiple rooms, group booking and waitlist workflows.
- Availability calendar with rooms available and nightly prices by room type and date; a calendar cell can start a booking.
- Tape chart with day/hour views and an option to auto-assign rooms to arrivals.
- Central Reservations searches availability across properties.
- Public direct-booking page can be configured for instant booking or request-to-book. Payment behavior is configurable and depends on its payment setup.
- Room blocks with reasons such as owner use, VIP, house use and maintenance.
- Group room blocks, pickup tracking and rooming lists.

### Front desk, rooms and guest service

- Front desk overview brings together arrivals, departures, in-house stays, occupancy, revenue indicators, open tasks, cash collected, outstanding balances and open folios.
- Room Board filters by floor and room type and shows availability, occupancy and housekeeping status. Room history and cleaning information are accessible from the board.
- In House organizes arrivals, current stays and departures by business date.
- Guest History records stays and lifetime value and supports search and export.
- Optional pre-check-in links and guest access instructions can be configured for arrivals.
- Housekeeping tasks include type, priority and status. Completing a task updates the room's current status; inspections after checkout cleaning can also be configured.
- Guest requests have deadlines/SLA reporting.
- When enabled, F&B includes restaurant POS, menu, kitchen orders/display, kitchen printers and outlet reporting. Banquets & Groups includes enquiries, functions, quotations and sales registers.

### Finance and end-of-day controls

- Folios show invoice status, totals, amounts paid and outstanding balances.
- Cashier module supports tills, cashier sessions, payment modes, cash counts and shift reports.
- Ledgers include guest, deposit, accounts-receivable, package and city-ledger aging views.
- Finance controls cover company billing terms, approval workflows for allowances, room taxes/fees, foreign currency and accounting export.
- Night Audit closes a property's business date, posts room/tax charges, handles no-show rules and produces an audit pack. It can be run manually or scheduled, with run history and exceptions.

### Management, AI and integrations

- Management reporting covers occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue, collections, budget comparisons, pickup/pace and source/segment mix.
- Department report packs and custom reports can be saved, shared and scheduled by email.
- Owner view offers a compact daily/monthly view of revenue, occupancy, money owed, near-term stays and recent audits.
- Sofia Agent can answer questions about the property's operation and, when configured and permitted, quote/book stays, check guests in/out, post charges/payments and work the waitlist. Access is governed by the signed-in user's roles and actions are recorded.
- Administration includes property setup, rooms and room-type pricing, staff/role access, activity log, module configuration, Marketplace, REST API, signed webhooks, AI/MCP connections and go-live import for bookings, companies and opening balances.

## What changed in the landing page

The previous page repeated the same claims in three sections: Benefits described reservations, less manual work, occupancy and team coordination; Features repeated reservations, check-in/out, guest records, availability, dashboard and tasks; How it works restated those as three generic steps. The audience section repeated the same operational benefits again.

The page now presents product capabilities once, in four interactive areas: Reservations, Operations, Finance, and Management & Intelligence. The Benefits and How it works sections were removed from the page, navigation and footer links were consolidated, and the audience section now focuses on property fit instead of repeating the feature list. The hero's example labels no longer use sample guest names and the mock interface is marked as illustrative.

## Claims that need care

- The interface exposes a booking engine and payment choices, but a specific payment gateway or live payment processing should only be advertised after its integration is confirmed for the Hotevo offer.
- The app has OTA booking sources and a Marketplace for channels, but the inspected screens do not prove that a particular channel manager or two-way OTA sync is enabled. Avoid claiming that without verifying the connector and the commercial plan.
- AI, F&B, banquets, webhooks and other areas depend on module configuration, provider setup and access roles. Keep the availability note on the landing page.
- No quantified savings, revenue lift, uptime promise or customer testimonial was established by this review.
- The site's contact form is currently a front-end concept: it validates fields locally and sends/stores nothing. Connect it to a real lead destination before relying on it for sales inquiries.

## Suggested next sales-page proof

When approved for public use, capture sanitized screenshots of the real availability calendar, Room Board, cashier/folio flow and management reports. Remove guest names, contact data, balances, property-specific details and credentials before using any capture in marketing.
